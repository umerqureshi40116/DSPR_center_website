About the centre

Dr. Sohaib and his team

DSPR is led by Dr. Sohaib Khalid, working alongside a team of male and female physiotherapists. Dr. Sohaib assesses and plans your care, and the team works from that one shared plan — so nothing gets lost between visits, and the plan keeps pace with your progress.

Sessions combine manual therapy, therapeutic modalities and supervised exercise inside the centre, backed by a home programme you can realistically keep up with. Female patients can be treated by a female therapist on request.
